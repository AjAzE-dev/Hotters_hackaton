# Connect

## Turning Organizational Knowledge into Human Connections

Large organizations are full of valuable knowledge, but finding the right person when you need an answer is often harder than finding the information itself. Expertise is scattered across emails, projects, documents, and conversations, making it difficult to know who is the best person to consult and whether their knowledge is current and relevant.

Connect is an AI-powered expertise discovery platform that helps employees identify the most qualified colleagues for any question.

Instead of returning another document or search result, Connect answers a more important question:

> "Who should I talk to?"

### How it works

An employee asks a question in natural language, such as:

> "Who has the most experience with Belgian payroll compliance?"

Connect analyzes organizational knowledge sources and builds an expertise map by linking employees to projects, topics, documents, and areas of expertise.

The platform then:

- Identifies the most relevant experts
- Ranks them using a confidence score
- Explains why they were selected
- Visualizes the relationships between people, projects, and knowledge domains

### Example Response

**Recommended Expert: Sarah Jacobs (94% Confidence)**

Why Sarah?

- Led 6 payroll-related projects
- Authored 3 payroll compliance documents
- Frequently referenced in payroll-related discussions
- Active in the field within the last 30 days

### Key Features

- 💬 Natural-language chat interface
- 🧠 AI-powered expertise discovery
- 🔍 Transparent reasoning and trust indicators
- 🌐 Interactive knowledge network visualization
- 📈 Confidence scoring based on experience and relevance

### Why Connect?

Today's challenge is not a lack of information. It is knowing:

- Who has the right expertise?
- Which knowledge is up to date?
- Who can be trusted to provide the best answer?

Connect transforms hidden organizational knowledge into visible, actionable expertise by connecting people with the colleagues most capable of helping them.

### Tech Concept

- Frontend: React + Vite
- AI Layer: LLM-powered query understanding
- Data Processing: Expertise extraction from organizational data
- Visualization: Interactive knowledge graph
- Deployment: Vercel

### Our Vision

Knowledge does not live in documents.

Knowledge lives in people.

Connect makes those people discoverable.
